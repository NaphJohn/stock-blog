> Lesson 2 of the *Fund Strategy* knowledge supplement. Maps to the email's "🔲 ETF Grid" and "ETF DCA Matrix" sections — first understand the ETF itself.

If you learn only one instrument, learn the **ETF (exchange-traded fund)**.

## 1. Index fund vs ETF

- **Index fund**: passively tracks an index (e.g. CSI 300) — "buy a basket in one click", diversifying single-stock blow-up risk.
- **ETF**: the exchange-traded version of an index fund — traded in your stock account in real time, **lower fees, more transparent, live pricing**.

In short: an ETF is the upgraded index fund, friendliest to retail.

## 2. Four ETF families

| Type | Example | Trait |
|---|---|---|
| Broad | CSI300 ETF (510300), CSI A500 ETF (563360) | Whole-market, moderate vol, DCA first choice |
| Sector | Semi / Pharma / Military ETF | Bet a theme, higher vol, satellite sleeve |
| Commodity | Gold ETF (518880) | Tracks gold, hedge + inflation |
| Cross-border | Nasdaq100 ETF (159501), S&P500 ETF (159655) | Overseas, watch premium & quota |

## 3. How to pick a good ETF?

Four hard metrics, by priority:

1. **Fees**: management + custody, broad ETFs often < 0.2%/yr — lower compounds in your favor;
2. **Tracking error**: smaller is better;
3. **Scale & liquidity**: bigger scale, higher daily turnover → tighter spreads, easier to exit;
4. **History**: 5+ years listed, consistently making new highs → steadier.

## 4. Why best for ordinary people?

- No need to study individual financials;
- Low fees, far cheaper than active funds long-term;
- Up over time (the economy grows) but volatile mid-way — exactly where **DCA + grid** turn volatility into an edge.

## 5. Two must-watch risks

- **Premium / discount**: common in cross-border ETFs; price may exceed (premium) or fall below (discount) NAV. Check **IOPV** before buying — skip if premium > 3%;
- **Quota / FX**: QDII types have FX quotas; when exhausted, subscriptions pause and premium spikes.

> Remember: an ETF is a *tool* — profits come from **disciplined use** (DCA base, grid enhancement, high-dividend ballast), not from predicting its direction.
