How to invest in bond funds during a bull market?

## Why one-shot buying in a bull market

For bond funds in a bull market we take a **one-shot buy** — why?

1. Bond funds basically keep rising through a bull market; quality bond funds are themselves long-term risers;
2. Quality bond funds are **positive ~75% of the time**, and in a bull market a pullback is unlikely.

So we can be a bit more aggressive — we only need its performance to be below the historical average, and we buy in one shot.

Because in a bull market funds usually outperform, what we earn is the **income from returning to normal returns through to the excess-return stage**.

## Specific buy conditions

① First tally the bond fund's historical average monthly return, historical max monthly return, and historical average annualised return;
② When **current return is below the historical average monthly return**, we can buy in one shot.

① When **year-to-date return > historical average annualised return**, sell 1/3;
② When **year-to-date return > the midpoint between the historical max and average annualised return**, sell 1/3;
③ When **year-to-date return > 90% of the historical maximum return**, clear the position.

The reason for clearing is, as always, to **respect history and patterns, and give up fantasy and luck**.
