Why do we stress cycle timing?

The vast majority of institutions don't time the market — they believe good managers and good assets rise over the long run, so finding quality assets, buying, and holding is the common practice.

But we must stress: **the goals of institutions and of us ordinary investors are not aligned**.

Institutions mainly earn management fees, while we ordinary investors mainly earn from our assets' price appreciation and dividends.

So **where you sit decides how you think — we must time the market!**

Also, for a good holding experience, we want to make money in as many different cycles as possible.

Further, **China's and the US's capital markets run on different underlying logic**.

Most of the time ours is a ranging market, not a long-term upward grind like the US — so buying in bears and selling in bulls is one of the core premises for earning excess return in this market.

Another premise: **even the best assets a manager picks still fluctuate with the cycle**.

So essentially, it's not that we don't believe in long-term value, but that we believe more in **cycle as one of the core laws of capital markets**.
